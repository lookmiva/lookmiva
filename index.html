01 / META-ARCHITECTURECONCEPTUAL SCHEMATIC

Layered thinking before the first instruction. A deliberate structure that considers context, boundaries, and how prompts may be interpreted or reused.

Clarity through structure

A design goal for resilient behavior: recognize conflicting instructions, reassert intended boundaries, and return to a constructive task. Effectiveness depends on implementation and testing.

Resilience as a discipline

Minimize unnecessary disclosure. Keep private source material outside public interfaces and reveal only what a task requires. Discretion is a design principle, not a promise of invisibility.

Less exposure by design

Human purpose at the center of every layer. Prioritize useful outcomes, clear boundaries, and responsible decisions. Security begins with what we choose to build.

Constructive intelligence

These layers describe a design philosophy. They are not a security certification or a guarantee against every attack.

THE DZAT PRINCIPLE

Beyond the form.Within the intention.

Dzat gives a name to our founding idea: the essential intention beneath an intelligent system. Not a claim of presence inside every model, but a commitment to carry the same principles across the work we create.

PRINCIPLE / 01

Discreet by design.

Meaningful outcomes need not reveal every internal instruction. Share the value. Respect the source.

PRINCIPLE / 02

Resilient by intention.

Question assumptions. Test the boundaries. Improve the architecture through evidence, not absolute promises.

PRINCIPLE / 03

Human at the core.

Intelligence earns its place by helping people. Constructive purpose is the center, not an afterthought.
